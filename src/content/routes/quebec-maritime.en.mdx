# Québec Maritime

A French-speaking adventure along the St. Lawrence: leave Montréal, wander the fortified streets of Québec City, then follow Route 132 as it loops around the Gaspé Peninsula. Camp at Forillon with the Gulf of St. Lawrence at your doorstep and keep binoculars ready for belugas and gannets.
