# Rocky Mountains Loop

Start in Calgary, cross the Continental Divide at Banff, follow the Icefields Parkway to Jasper and finish on the Pacific coast in Vancouver. This is the route that sells the RV dream: glacier-fed lakes, elk at roadside pullouts, and full-hookup campgrounds with mountain views.

**Tip from our team:** book campgrounds at Lake Louise months in advance for July and August, and keep a 40 L jerry can for the longer stretches between fuel stops.
