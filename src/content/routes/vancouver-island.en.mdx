# Vancouver Island Explorer

A relaxed loop that fits perfectly in a Class B camper van: catch the ferry from Tsawwassen or Horseshoe Bay, explore Victoria, then drive the Pacific Coast Highway to Tofino. Watch for Roosevelt elk in Pacific Rim and book a sunset surf session at Cox Bay.
